UCDegens Power Rankings:  
Week 10 
NOVEMBER 11, 2026 / DRAFT FOR COMMISSIONER REVIEW 
The record is real. So are the injury reports. 
Week 9 gave us a 148.10 from Puka's team, another 143.28 from Hertz, and two teams sitting at 7-2. It also 
left the hottest recent scorer figuring out how to replace Puka. The standings remember the points you 
already scored. These rankings also ask who is available to score next week. 
Five regular-season weeks remain and eight teams make the playoffs. Hertz keeps the top spot, Kousins 
and Jerry are almost inseparable, and the middle is still packed.  
Quick takeaways 
Hertz stays first. Best all-play record, strongest Week 10 projected lineup, and best remaining scoring 
outlook. 
Second and third are a virtual tie. Kousins leads Jerry by less than 0.05 power-score points. Do not 
overread the order. 
SWKL climbs to fourth. A 7-2 record helps, injuries still keep the team on their toes but they manage to 
win week after week.  
Puka’s high powered engine blows up: Its 140.99 last-three PPG is best in the league; injuries and byes 
drive the drop to ninth. 
Plan the empty slots. The current roster model leaves several teams without a starter in a later week. 
Those holes are fixable.

The top 4 
01 / HertzTushRentals 
POWER 112.11 | 6-3 | No change from Week 9 
The No. 1 spot survives another week, and this time there is less room for debate. A 143.28 against 
Daddymundo moves Hertz to 6-3. It has the best all-play record, the most points scored, a strong lineup-
efficiency mark, and the highest projected remaining scoring. There is a difference between getting hot and 
having the roster to stay hot. This team is doing both. 
Why here: First in all-play, next-week lineup strength, and remaining projected scoring. No apparent holes 
in the team.  
Weakness: No strong weakness, stay healthy and this team strives. Tyler Warren is a volatile TE with the 
uncertainty of the Colts offense.  
How to improve: Use depth pieces to take a stab at another star before the deadline and optimize for 
playoffs.  
 
02 / KirK Kousins 
POWER 108.75 | 6-3 | Up 1 from Week 9 
The spreadsheet moves Kousins to second, but this is a photo finish: the margin over Jerry is less than 
0.05 power-score points. A 118.16 win gets the record to 6-3. The real strength is the full-season 
combination of scoring, an excellent all-play record, and the league's highest lineup efficiency. The 
manager keeps getting the points off the bench and onto the scoreboard. 
Why here: The second-best all-play record and second-best remaining scoring outlook, plus the best lineup 
efficiency. Those edges barely outweigh Jerry's better record and softer schedule.  
Weakness: The Bowers question is now open, we hope that Mendoza can keep up with the historic season 
Bowers has had. Has a lot of flex worthy RBs but no true RB2 on the team. 
How to improve: Arrange a trade to offload some of the bench RBs to get a low end RB2 or a better flex 
play.

03 / Jerry Jones’s Auction 
POWER 108.71 | 7-2 | Down 1 from Week 9 
Jerry gets the win back, 110.76 to 93.16 over aa3345, and remains tied for the best record at 7-2. The 
recent scoring is less convincing: 103.44 per week over the last three, tenth in the league. This is still a top-
three roster with an exceptionally friendly remaining schedule. It is also a reminder that a great record does 
not make the weekly scoring automatic. 
Why here: The joint-best record, a strong Week 10 lineup, and the easiest remaining schedule with him 
playing Vikram twice. Kousins edges ahead on efficiency and the remaining scoring outlook. 
Weakness: Uncertainty, the team relies on the effectiveness of Brissett, if Chase Brown can start scoring 
touchdowns, if the Jaguars increase the passing volume, and if Kittle can maintain that volume with the 
Niners receiving core getting healthy.  
How to improve: Possibly pair the big name pieces to get a more reliable every week starter or take a big 
risk with playoffs around the corner as this level of scoring will not win playoff games.  
 
04 / SWKL 
POWER 105.37 | 7-2 | Up 1 from Week 9 
A 129.64 win over Im ovulating keeps SWKL at 7-2. The record is excellent, the scoring differential is 
strong, and the recent average is healthy. It finally climbs from fifth to fourth. The all-play record still trails 
the other top teams, though, and the full remaining lineup outlook is closer to league average than the win-
loss column makes it look. 
Why here: The joint-best record, a strong point differential, and 125.83 PPG over the last three weeks. The 
all-play record supports a good team, even if it does not quite support the top spot. Burrow and Bijan being 
one of the strongest carries in the league with the both of them having the ability to score 50+ combined on 
any given night.  
Weakness: Lack of a reliable RB2, Pickens having a lingering injury, Nico Collins having to fight for targets 
in a crowded receiving room.  
Watch out: The Week 11 lineup needs Roschon Johnson and Rachaad White in the backfield. That is a 
much different week from simply penciling Bijan into the lineup. 
How to improve: Trade one of the 3 WRs to get a reliable RB2.

05 / Im ovulating 
POWER 102.50 | 4-5 | Down 1 from Week 9  
The name changed; the frustrating gap between the roster and the record did not. An 87.06 against SWKL 
makes it 4-5, but the 58-41 all-play record is tied with SWKL. That scoring would imply about 5.27 wins 
against an average schedule. The projected lineup is still good enough to push back into the playoff picture, 
provided the latest low week does not become a habit. 
Why here: The all-play record and remaining lineup outlook carry a below-.500 team above the middle. The 
model rates the roster more highly than the actual record. 
Weakness: High projections, low floor for JT. Jacobs has been great ppg wise but run game leaves Jacobs 
relying on the passing game. Bad bench with Ladd, Hampton, and possibly Tre Tucker stinking up the 
bench. Woody Marks will give you the cleanest 9.5 points, nothing more, nothing less.  
How to improve: Consider trading JSN for depth if depth woes continue as a consistent 15 and 15 will be 
less stressful than hoping JSN gets 25 to offset the Flex 5.  
 
06 / aa3345 
POWER 101.70 | 4-5 | Up 1 from Week 9 
Two straight losses have the record at 4-5, but the current lineup still looks stronger than the record. The 
catch is that the all-play record is only 47-52: this is not a dominant team being robbed every week. It is a 
capable roster that needs to turn the good names into a steadier weekly score. 
Why here: The Week 10 lineup and remaining scoring outlook lift the team above average. The losing 
record and below-.500 all-play record push in the other direction. 
Weakness: Drake London continues to underperform with Penix and has a tough 3 weeks now. RB room 
looks a little shallow with Warren being the only clear ROS play.  
How to improve: The depth is thinner but find other people desperate for a WR to offload Drake London 
onto.

07 / Maye Chase McCooks 
POWER 100.52 | 5-4 | Down 1 from Week 9 
The 125.66 loss to Puka drops Maye to 5-4, but there is no collapse here. The last-three-week average is 
123.82 and the point differential remains positive. The more cautious reading comes from the 49-50 all-play 
record and an ordinary remaining lineup projection. There is enough to compete, but the weekly floor 
depends heavily on getting the supporting spots right. 
Why here: A winning record, positive point differential, and good recent scoring. The all-play record and 
current roster outlook keep the power score close to the league average. 
Weakness: Lineup efficiency is below average. The RB2 has become a streamer play each week with 
Aaron Jones splitting the backfield now. WR2 is more dicey with Egbuka and Boston being iffy plays and 
McBride has the unknown of a rookie QB and the expectation of defenses to just swarm McBride each play.  
How to improve: Possibly sell high on McBride for a good RB2 or WR2 and stream TEs.  
 
08 / Brandon Aubrey Sinclair 
POWER 100.15 | 4-5 | Up 1 from Week 9 
A 109.24 win gets Brandon to 4-5 and into eighth in these rankings. The all-play record is a little above 
.500, so the actual record still undersells the season slightly. The backfield is a real foundation. The 
remaining lineup average, however, stays below 120.00, which leaves little room for shaky receiving or 
tight-end weeks. 
Why here: A slightly positive all-play record and a Week 10 lineup that grades above the league average. 
The negative point differential and losing record hold the score near 100. 
Weakness: AJ Brown, hopefully Cam Ward still plays good 
How to improve: Look for one dependable receiver as AJ Brown and Tate are more volatile  
09 / Puka Me Up to Heaven 
POWER 95.57 | 5-4 | Down 1 from Week 9 
The strangest profile in the league: three-week scoring leader at 140.99, a 148.10 win in Week 9, and a 5-4 
record - yet ninth in power due to injuries to Wilson and Puka.  
Why here: The best momentum score and a winning record help. The lowest Week 10 lineup projection 
and a bottom-two remaining scoring outlook hurt much more under the published weights. 
Weakness: Immediate WR depth. The projected Week 10 lineup leans on Konata Mumpfield and KC 
Concepcion; the current Week 11 roster cannot fill the second WR slot. 
How to improve: Find a healthy receiver who can start in Week 11 as well as now. Keep the useful RB 
depth unless a trade clearly improves the starting lineup. The target is survival through the byes, not 
replacing Puka with one desperate deal.

10 / Fuck You Navin 
POWER 92.74 | 2-7 | Up 1 from Week 9 | Previously I'm Gonna Nix Myself 
The record is 2-7, but the rest of the profile is less hopeless. The all-play record implies about 3.36 wins, so 
there has been some schedule pain. A 102.68 loss to Brandon does not help the comeback, though. 
Jefferson, Moore, Irving, and Hubbard give the future lineups some substance. The challenge is turning that 
projected talent into wins before the calendar runs out. 
Why here: Recent scoring and the remaining lineup outlook are stronger than those of the bottom two 
teams. Seven losses and a poor all-play record still carry a large penalty. 
Weakness: Flex play is very shaky, DJ Moore could have low nights as well, needs a reliable QB that can 
get 17-18 points easily in any week.  
How to improve: Trade Waddle away for a possible decent QB. Continue to play the waiver wire to get a 
good flex play. And optimize to enter the playoffs, not win the championship. With jjettas being viable now, 
each week matters alongside PF for tiebreakers. 
11 / Chase Brown Kids 
POWER 89.55 | 2-7 | Down 1 from Week 9 
A 110.98 loss to Kousins was more competitive than the two weeks before it, but the record is now 2-7. The 
last-three-week average is still only 90.63, and lineup efficiency remains the lowest in the league. The 
projected roster is not the worst. That distinction matters only if the manager turns the available players into 
a consistently legal, useful starting lineup. 
Why here: The remaining lineup outlook is better than the record suggests, but the losses, all-play record, 
recent scoring, and efficiency all pull the score down. 
Weakness: Depth, on any given bye week, there isn’t a strong person next up. Likely hasn’t looked good 
since JJ became QB and Laporta hasn’t been playing well either since the contract talks.  
How to improve: Possibly trade Downs high before he plays, trade likely or laporta to a team needing a 
TE.  
12 / Daddymundo 
POWER 82.32 | 2-7 | No change from Week 9 | Previously Bye Week 
A new name cannot hide a 68.12 against Hertz. Daddymundo is 2-7, last in all-play, last in point differential, 
and last in recent scoring. The 24-75 all-play record says this is mostly a scoring problem rather than a run 
of unlucky opponents. Barkley and a healthier receiving group can improve individual weeks, but the roster 
still needs basic coverage before it needs a miracle. 
Why here: He sucks 
Weakness: Doesn’t utilize the waiver wire, injury plagued, bad start/sits 
How to improve: Trade Saquon high to get a good WR, RB room is actually not terrible and with another 
usable WR. The team could spoil some games in this last 5 game stretch.
