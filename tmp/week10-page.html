The record is real. So are the injury reports.

The week at a glance +

Five regular-season weeks remain and eight teams make the playoffs. Hertz keeps the top spot, Kousins and Jerry are almost inseparable, and the middle is still packed.

  • Hertz stays first. Best all-play record, strongest Week 10 projected lineup, and best remaining scoring outlook.
  • Second and third are a virtual tie. Kousins leads Jerry by less than 0.05 power-score points. Do not overread the order.
  • SWKL climbs to fourth. A 7-2 record helps; injuries still keep the team on their toes, but they manage to win week after week.
  • Puka’s high-powered engine blows up. Its 140.99 last-three PPG is best in the league; injuries and byes drive the drop to ninth.
  • Plan the empty slots. The current roster model leaves several teams without a starter in a later week. Those holes are fixable.
About this edition +

These are the November 11 power rankings entering Week 10, with results through Week 9. Team names and writeups are preserved from the supplied edition.

Lineup points use the best legal projected starting combination for Week 10. Remaining scoring averages that calculation independently across Weeks 10–14. Players on bye are excluded, empty slots score 0.00, and future pickups or trades are not assumed.

Roster coverage lists uncovered starting positions at that snapshot, rather than predicting that managers will leave them empty. All-play compares each team against every other team each week. Luck is actual wins minus expected wins from its all-play win percentage.
